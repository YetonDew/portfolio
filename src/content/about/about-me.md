Two years ago I moved to Poland, and honestly, it’s been a journey full of surprises. I’ve always enjoyed learning new things, but this experience really pushed me to learn something everyday. From dealing with cultural differences to trying to learn one of the hardest languagues in the worl, Polish.

I had the oportunity to became a student at 42 Warsaw, and it’s been such a unique experience. It’s all about learning by doing and sharing knowledge with others. You can be chatting over coffee one moment and suddenly end up discussing code or different ways to solve a problem. I’ve met amazing people from all over the world who share the same curiosity (and slight obsession) for coding.

At the same time, I’ve been working as a dance teacher. Something I never expected, but that I’ve grown to love. It’s helped me build a lot of soft skills like communication, confidence, leading groups, and just being comfortable interacting with people.

All of this has shaped how I see things: at the end of the day, tech is about people. We build things for people, and with people. So yeah, learning to code is important, but learning how to connect, communicate, and understand others matters just as much.
